## A higher-order proposal: useful computation within ecological limits

One proposal is to make useful service within ecological and social limits the
goal, rather than treating additional computation as a benefit in itself. In
Meadows's twelve-point framework, this involves goals (3), paradigms (2), rules
(5), information flows (6) and, only after allocation is justified, numerical
parameters (12). These classifications identify kinds of intervention; they do
not prove that a proposal will work or rank its expected value.

The planetary-boundaries framework does not supply a unique permissible compute
budget. Translating global limits into sector, operator and site allocations
requires explicit scientific assumptions and distributive choices. A workable
proposal would keep lifecycle greenhouse-gas limits separate from basin/season
water constraints, local ecosystem protection and hardware/material impacts.
A scalar FLOP allowance is not an adequate proxy for all of them.

Norway is a siting hypothesis, not a demonstrated optimum. NVE identifies favorable
conditions and power/grid constraints. Site comparison must account for marginal
power, additional infrastructure, seasonal water, habitat, local rights, heat
reuse, service needs and resilience. Relocation must not conceal displaced costs
or bypass affected people. An infrastructure constraint also does not, on its
own, establish control over all future autonomous AI activity.

Permacomputing supplies complementary demand reduction: avoid unnecessary work,
reuse results, profile deterministic paths before invoking models, choose smaller
models where quality allows, defer flexible loads, and extend useful hardware
life. Measure absolute resource demand as well as intensity; efficiency gains
can be consumed by growth. Test service quality, rebound and burden shifting.

Independent review should join Earth-system allocation specialists, energy/water
and lifecycle engineers, AI evaluators, rights-holders and affected communities.
The open questions are what can be measured and enforced, who allocates the
budget, who may challenge it, and what happens when an operator exceeds it.
No allocation, site ranking or enforceable budget has been calculated here.
