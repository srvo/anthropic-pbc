# Adversarial review, 2026-11-04

Four independent reviewers read the repository at `a83255b` (branch
`ec-ai-corpus`), each from one angle, and tried to break it:

1. A stranger rebuilding from a fresh clone.
2. Number integrity in the built deck.
3. Structure and documentation.
4. Method and publication risk.

The reviewers were agents. They ran commands in scratch clones and did not
touch the working tree. Every finding below gives its evidence. "Ran" means
the reviewer confirmed the finding by executing something; "read" means it
was found by reading files. H5 was re-checked by hand: the transcript text
columns are present.

Open decisions raised here are in Praxis `OPENQUESTIONS.md`, Q24–Q26.

## High

**H1. "137 firms raised AI as a risk" counts analysts' words as the firms'.**
`scripts/build_edition.py:237-266` reads `ai-windows.parquet` with no speaker
role filter. Counting only company speakers (`issuer_heuristic`) changes the
numbers (ran):

| | All speakers | Company speakers only |
|---|---|---|
| Firms | 137 | 79 |
| Cyber | 40 | 20 |
| Safety | 31 | 14 |
| Capex | 26 | 14 |

The dialogue-space slide separates executives from analysts, so the deck
contradicts itself.

**H2. The lead chart's rise is mostly growth in AI talk, not a change in
framing.** "12.2% of 2026 calls, against at most 0.6% before 2023" is a
call-level rate: a call counts if any of its AI windows is flagged. AI windows
per call grew from about 1 in 2022 to about 5.7 in 2026. With any constant
false-positive rate, the call-level rate rises with volume alone. Per AI
window, the rate is roughly flat through 2024 (read,
`proposals/earnings-calls/codebook-v0.1/report/by-year.tsv`). That table uses
codebook v0.1 and fiscal years; the deck uses v0.2-draft and calendar years,
so the shape transfers but the values do not:

| Fiscal year | Rate per AI window |
|---|---|
| 2022 | 0.8% |
| 2023 | 1.0% |
| 2024 | 1.0% |
| 2025 | 1.5% |
| 2026 | 3.3% |

Any real shift shows only in 2025–26, and it is unverified until the gold set
is coded. → Q26.

**H3. The deck publishes rates its own spec keeps in `proposals/`.**

- `docs/bert-enrichment-spec.md` §10 says no count "is citable as a rate of
  anything except witness agreement" until precision and recall are measured.
- `gold/earnings-calls/codebook-v0.1/score.tsv`: 0 of 240 rows coded.
- The captions state rates as findings; a grey tag on slide 1 does not undo
  that.

(read) → Q16.

**H4. The dialogue-space slide compares levels the spec says are not
comparable.**

- The spec allows HN-to-call comparison "only in rough order".
- The 61% for Wikipedia is partly built in: v0.2's new items were read from
  Wikipedia's AI-risk headings and then scored on those same articles.
- The executives' cells rest on 179 units.
- The three sources use different date windows, hand-typed in
  `scripts/v02_map.py:41-50` and not tied to the as-of date. The caption does
  not say so.

(read)

**H5. Verbatim Yahoo-sourced transcript text is redistributed.**

- `corpus/earnings-calls/ai-paragraphs.parquet` has a `content` column (28,602
  paragraphs with speaker names) (re-checked).
- `ai-windows.parquet` has a `text` column (38,600 rows) (re-checked).
- `ai-questions.parquet` holds 4,288 full analyst turns.
- The only licence is the uploader's ODC-BY tag on Hugging Face. ODC-BY
  covers database rights, not copyright in the transcripts. The defeatbeta
  card says the data comes from Yahoo Finance and is "intended for research
  and educational purposes".
- The same text also sits outside the `corpus/` carve-out, so it is implicitly
  CC0:
  - `proposals/earnings-calls/codebook-v0.1/report/risk-quotes-ttm.tsv`
  - `oversight-questions.tsv`
  - `gold/earnings-calls/*/sample.tsv`
- `.zenodo.json` (`cc-zero`) and `CITATION.cff` (`CC0-1.0`) label the whole
  deposit CC0.

The Hacker News pattern (ids, offsets, hashes, rebuild from the pin) would
remove the exposure. → Q24.

## Medium

**M1. `--check` can never pass at an edition commit.** The stamp and notice
embed HEAD (`build_edition.py:204,498,513`), and committing the edition moves
HEAD. As written, a freshness check in CI cannot pass (ran). Fix: compare
against the stamp's `edition_of`, or keep the commit out of the compared
bytes.

**M2. The numeral lint checks membership, not identity.**
`allowed()` (`build_edition.py:152-156`) accepts any numeral that matches any
registered value: 46 of the integers 0–100 pass. It also misses these (ran):

- Minus signs are dropped.
- Spelled-out numbers, fractions such as ½, and superscripts produce no
  tokens.
- `STATIC_TOKENS` "500" passes anywhere, not only in "S&P 500".

**M3. Some channels go unchecked** (read, regex tests):

- HTML comments, which Marp ships as PPTX presenter notes.
- Image alt text.
- Front matter.
- SVG `<tspan>` text, tail text and `aria-label`.
- Matplotlib tick labels, which include the "(to 11-04)" as-of date.

**M4. Quotes and provenance are loosely checked.**

- The quote check covers straight double quotes only; curly quotes, single
  quotes and blockquotes evade it (ran).
- `q_33k` "33,000+" matches the paper's paraphrase. The paper's own table
  gives the claim as ">33,000 critical- and high-severity flaws" and marks it
  UNVERIFIED.
- Registry `source`/`how` strings are free text.
- Copied keys (`ds_exec_safety`, `ec_pct_witness_last`) have no n/d in the
  stamp.

**M5. Two captions overstate.**

- **Intent slide.** "21.2% sells vs 0.8% controls" sets the largest
  commercial intent against the smallest oversight one. The group totals are
  32.2% vs 5.7% (529 and 94 of 1645), about 5.6×, not 26×.
- **HN slide.** "Cyber dominates HN's AI-security set (85%)" is close to true
  by construction: that set is selected on security terms.

(ran)

**M6. The CVE footnote uses the earnings-call as-of date as its baseline**

refreshed alone, the footnote is silently wrong.

## Low

- **Unchecked files.**
  - `scripts/__pycache__/` and `.claude/worktrees/` are not gitignored.
  - `.claude/settings.json` and `.entire/settings.json` are tracked, against
    AGENTS.md's "never stage `.claude/`".
- **Unused convention.** AGENTS.md says to cite data by step URI; no doc does.
- **Repo size.** The tracked tree is 110 MB after 53 commits. The model-output
  parquets (up to 7 MB each) grow history with every re-witness.
- **Model scores.** `hn_report.py:136` casts float32 scores to double before
  `display.score`, so a few examples round differently from their float32
  value (for example 0.38595 → 0.3859).
- **Undefined values shown as zero.** `hn_report.py:171-172` prints 0 for an
  undefined rate (d = 0).
- **Private references in docs.**
  - `docs/earnings-call-ai-corpus.md:162` names a private vault key.
  - Four `meta.yml` files name `github.com/srvo/anthropic-pbc` as the public
    home while Q13 is open.
- **Rendering.** The PDF and PPTX renders are not byte-identical between runs
  (Q18). The chart images have no alt text (Q23).
