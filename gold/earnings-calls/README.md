# Earnings-call AI gold set

A hand-coded sample of AI windows from `corpus/earnings-calls/ai-windows.parquet`.
It scores the two NLI witnesses (DeBERTa, ModernBERT) and the lexical "risk"
measure against human labels, so their precision and recall become known
rather than assumed ([`docs/earnings-call-ai-corpus.md`](../../docs/earnings-call-ai-corpus.md) §7).

**Labels are human-only.** [`docs/bert-enrichment-spec.md`](../../docs/bert-enrichment-spec.md)
§9 bars labels generated by any LLM. Every label in `sample.tsv` is typed by a
person, whose name is in the `coder` column.

## Files

- `codebook-v0.1/sample.tsv`: 240 windows drawn deterministically by
  `scripts/ec_gold.py sample`. Strata and allocation are in the script docstring.
  The file carries no model score, so coding is blind.
- `codebook-v0.1/score.tsv`: written by `score`. It gives precision and recall
  per hypothesis and witness, stratum-weighted, with n beside every rate.

## How to code

    uv run scripts/ec_gold.py code --coder YOUR_NAME

Each window is shown with its symbol, date, speaker and role and its verbatim
text. Answer `y` or `n` and press Enter. `s` skips the row; it is offered again
next run. `q` quits. The file is saved after every row, so you can stop and
resume at any time. Risk types are asked only when `ai_risk` is `y`: type their
numbers (for example `1 7`), or press Enter if none fits. Then score with:

    uv run scripts/ec_gold.py score

## Label definitions

**Code what the speaker says, not what you think of AI.** A label is `y` when
the window's text states it, `n` otherwise. Use `note` for anything a later
reader needs to understand the call.

The definitions are the hypotheses in
[`corpus/earnings-calls/codebook-v0.1.tsv`](../../corpus/earnings-calls/codebook-v0.1.tsv), verbatim:

| Label | Definition |
|---|---|
| `about_ai` | This text is about artificial intelligence or machine learning. |
| `ai_risk` | This text describes a risk, threat, cost, or downside of artificial intelligence. |
| `governance` | This text describes how the company governs, oversees, or controls its use of artificial intelligence. |

`risk_types` is a semicolon list drawn from:

| id | Definition |
|---|---|
| `regulation` | This text discusses legal or regulatory risk from artificial intelligence, such as AI laws, compliance, or government action. |
| `cyber` | This text discusses cybersecurity threats, fraud, or attacks that use or target artificial intelligence. |
| `privacy` | This text discusses data privacy or data protection concerns related to artificial intelligence. |
| `accuracy` | This text discusses artificial intelligence making errors, hallucinating, or producing unreliable outputs. |
| `bias` | This text discusses bias, fairness, or discrimination in artificial intelligence systems. |
| `labor` | This text discusses job losses, workforce reductions, or lower headcount because of artificial intelligence. |
| `disruption` | This text discusses artificial intelligence disrupting or threatening the company's own business or competitive position. |
| `capex` | This text discusses the cost of artificial intelligence investment or uncertain returns on AI spending. |
| `energy` | This text discusses the electricity, power, or energy demands of artificial intelligence and data centers. |
| `supply` | This text discusses shortages or supply constraints for AI chips, GPUs, or data-center capacity. |
| `ip` | This text discusses intellectual property, copyright, or content licensing issues related to artificial intelligence. |
| `safety_ethics` | This text discusses ethical, reputational, safety, or misuse concerns about artificial intelligence. |

`ai_opportunity` is not coded in this round.

## Analyst questions

A second set: 200 analyst questions from `corpus/earnings-calls/ai-questions.parquet`,
in `codebook-questions-v0.1/`. It tests the claim that oversight questions are
rare next to commercial ones. Strata and allocation are in the `ec_gold.py`
docstring. Code with:

    uv run scripts/ec_gold.py --set questions code --coder YOUR_NAME
    uv run scripts/ec_gold.py --set questions score

**Code what the analyst asks, not what you think of AI.** All 10 labels are asked
for every question; answer each `y` or `n`. Definitions are verbatim from
[`corpus/earnings-calls/codebook-questions-v0.1.tsv`](../../corpus/earnings-calls/codebook-questions-v0.1.tsv):

| Label | Group | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| `q_revenue` | commercial | This question asks about revenue, sales, demand, orders, or pricing from artificial intelligence. |
| `q_efficiency` | commercial | This question asks about using artificial intelligence to cut costs or improve efficiency or margins. |
| `q_spend` | capital | This question asks about spending or investment on artificial intelligence, or the return on that spending. |
| `q_competition` | threat | This question asks whether artificial intelligence threatens the company's business, customers, or competitive position. |
| `q_workforce` | workforce | This question asks about the effect of artificial intelligence on jobs, employees, or headcount. |
| `q_risk` | oversight | This question asks about the risks or downsides of artificial intelligence. |
| `q_controls` | oversight | This question asks how the company oversees, controls, governs, or manages the risks of its artificial intelligence. |
| `q_regulation` | oversight | This question asks about regulation, legal liability, or compliance related to artificial intelligence. |
| `q_harm` | oversight | This question asks about harm that artificial intelligence could cause to customers, users, or society, such as errors, bias, or misuse. |
| `q_security` | oversight | This question asks about security, privacy, or data protection risks of artificial intelligence. |

`score` reports each label and the `oversight` and `commercial` groups. A group
counts as positive when any of its labels is `y`.
